# Gemsteak Design System
Whimsical 2D vector indie-game studio aesthetic (tower-defense / fantasy RPG title screen vibe).
Brand motif: a steak cut whose "meat" is faceted ruby crystal and whose "rind" is faceted grey stone.

## Color
- Backgrounds: twilight gradients — deep magical blue (#1b1446 → #2b1d6b), pastel purple (#8f7bd8), starry night sky.
- Accents: ruby red (#e0234e), neon pink (#ff4fa3), glowing cyan (#3ff3ff).
- Surfaces: cool grey stone (#8a93a6) and slate blue (#4f5d80) with faceted geometric shading (light top-left facet, dark bottom-right facet).
- Outline: pure black #0b0b12.

## Shape & Line
- Every container, button and card has a THICK hand-drawn black outline (4px solid black) like cartoon sprites.
- Hard offset drop shadows (6px 6px 0 #0b0b12), no soft blur shadows except for glows.
- Faceted / chamfered corners (clip-path polygons) on gems and stone panels.

## Typography
- Headings: chunky, bold, playful display font, RPG title-screen style, with black stroke and a ruby/pink drop shadow.
- Body: clean, highly legible rounded sans-serif.

## Interaction
- Hover: crystals glow (cyan/pink outer glow, slight brightness lift); stone buttons depress (translate 4px down, shadow shrinks) like a gamepad button.
